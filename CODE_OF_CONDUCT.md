# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — critique the code, never the person
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when an individual
represents the project in public. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate them, and may temporarily or permanently
ban a contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, open a **private security advisory** or contact the maintainers through their GitHub profile.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
